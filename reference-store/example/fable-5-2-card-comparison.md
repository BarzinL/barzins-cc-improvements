# Opus 5.5 against Fable 5.2 in the Fable 5.2 system card

Scope: what Anthropic's system card for Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2
(published 2026-11-09) reports about Opus 5.5 relative to Fable 5.2 on long
engineering and knowledge-work benchmarks. Page numbers are the printed page footers.

## Claims

- On FrontierSWE v2, Fable 5.2 scored 69.9% and Opus 5.5 scored 62.3%.
  [src: https://assets.claude.com/27d60918d218125e7504bc7354c4b4626ec56b11.pdf | read: 2026-11-14 | status: source | loc: §8.7, p. 212]
- The card says Fable 5.2's gains over Opus 5.5 on FrontierSWE v2 "were greatest for
  performance optimization, scientific computing, and visual reasoning".
  [src: https://assets.claude.com/27d60918d218125e7504bc7354c4b4626ec56b11.pdf | read: 2026-11-14 | status: source | loc: §8.7, p. 212]
- FrontierSWE v2 has 34 long engineering and research tasks, gives each trial a
  20-hour budget, and runs every model at max reasoning effort in Proximal's own agent
  harness.
  [src: https://www.frontierswe.com/blog/v2 | read: 2026-11-14 | status: secondary | via: the Fable 5.2 system card, §8.7, p. 212]
- On AA-Briefcase v1.1 at max effort, Opus 5.5 had an Elo of 1800 and Fable 5.2 1785;
  on rubric pass rate Fable 5.2 scored 66.1% and Opus 5.5 61.2%.
  [src: https://assets.claude.com/27d60918d218125e7504bc7354c4b4626ec56b11.pdf | read: 2026-11-14 | status: source | loc: §8.14.4, p. 239]
